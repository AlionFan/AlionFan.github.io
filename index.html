TrainBrain: focus and memory training website

TrainBrain is a structured cognitive training and performance analysis platform organized around practice, challenges, dashboards, rankings, and a personal profile.

Turning training into a system you can keep advancing

34 levels · 7 cognitive categories · XP and achievements

From the public pages, TrainBrain uses attention, memory, logic and calculation, reaction, executive function, processing speed, and spatial ability as training paths, with level challenges, daily goals, leveling, and leaderboards to keep progress visible.
